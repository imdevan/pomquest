## the dungeon cycle

1. the party enters the "location"
2. the party enters a room or hall
  - maybe they are all Rooms of varying types
3. "Encounter" something
- create book_of_encounters
  - hall: 
      door: 50%
      fight: 20%
      trap
      hidden treasure
      fake wall
      left
      right
      fork
  - room: 
      fight: 75%
      puzzle
      nothing
      trap

  - defeat: loot
  - lose: follow tpk 
  - retreat? - would have to involve remember remaining enemies in room. 
4. "Explore" the room
5. Roll for Doors
  - open / unlock
  - potentially trapped
6. Repeat from 2      
7. Dungeon Ending - story beat | elite | disappointment | escape certain death | boss - if at end of quest cycle

### encounter

#### fights

1. roll for challenge rating adjustment
  factor: avg party level, depth into dungeon
2. generate enemies from book_of_monsters 
  based on challenge rating budget, location biome and depth into dungeon
3. begin round

##### round
turn order decided randomly each round. each hero "rolls" 1d6 + dex
enemies attack after heroes.. not shown in log

###### turn

on heros turn the hero card becomes "active"

hero attacks based on stats

hero rolls 2d6+atk_mod to determine if attack hits
  - use nerd font icons to represent d6 rolls in quest log 󰇏
  - roll to beat enemy dc 
  - on 1,1 critical miss hero takes 1 dmg
  - on 6,6 critical hit hero deals max dmg
